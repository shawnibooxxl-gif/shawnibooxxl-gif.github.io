LOAD VAULT

Privacy Policy

Effective November 17, 2026

The short version: Load Vault does not collect, send, or sell any of your information, and it does not connect to the internet. Everything you enter stays on your phone.

Who we are

Load Vault is a reloading log app published by Big Bear LLC, doing business as Big Bear Inc. ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the Android app does with your information.

Information we collect

None. Load Vault has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking tools, and it does not connect to the internet.

What is stored on your phone

Your load records, range sessions, brass lots, component stock, and notes are saved only in the app's own storage on your device. This data is not uploaded to us or to anyone else.

Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes this information from your phone.

Permissions

Load Vault asks for no device permissions. It does not use your location, camera, photos, contacts, or microphone.

Exports

If you export your load book as a PDF, Load Vault opens your phone's share or print menu and you choose where it goes. Once you send it, that service's own privacy policy applies to the file.

Purchases

If you buy Load Vault, the payment is handled by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details.

Safety notice

Load Vault is a record-keeping tool. It does not provide load data. You are responsible for the data you enter and for following published reloading manuals.

Children

Load Vault is not directed at children under 13, and it does not collect personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date above.
