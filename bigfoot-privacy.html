CAN YOU FIND BIGFOOT?

Privacy Policy

Effective November 17, 2026

The short version: Can You Find Bigfoot? does not collect, send, or sell any of your information. Your progress stays on your phone.

Who we are

Can You Find Bigfoot? is a search-and-find game published by Big Bear LLC, doing business as Big Bear Inc. ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the Android game does with your information.

Information we collect

None. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking tools. It does not send any information off your phone.

What is stored on your phone

Your stars, best times, and settings such as sound and Kids Mode are saved in the game's own storage on your device so you can pick up where you left off. This data is not uploaded to us or to anyone else.

Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes this information from your phone.

Permissions

The game asks for no device permissions. It does not use your location, camera, photos, contacts, or microphone.

Links to our books

The game includes links to our Hidden Cryptids coloring books on Amazon. Tapping one opens Amazon in your browser or the Amazon app. The game sends nothing to Amazon, and what you do there is covered by Amazon's privacy policy.

Children

The game does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date above.
