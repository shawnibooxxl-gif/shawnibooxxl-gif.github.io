GRAIL CASE

Privacy Policy

Effective November 13, 2026

The short version: Grail Case does not collect, send, or sell any of your information. Everything you enter stays on your phone.

Who we are

Grail Case is a collection tracker app published by Big Bear LLC, doing business as Big Bear Inc. ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the Android app does with your information.

Information we collect

None. Grail Case has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking tools. We do not receive anything you enter into the app, and we could not see your collection even if we wanted to.

What is stored on your phone

The pieces you add, including names, details, prices, values, notes, and photos, are saved only in the app's own storage on your device. This data is not uploaded to us or to anyone else.

Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes this information from your phone. You can also erase everything from inside the app with Settings > Delete everything.

Camera and photos

Grail Case asks for camera or photo access only when you choose to add a picture of a piece. The pictures you pick are copied into the app's storage on your phone and are not sent anywhere. You can decline or remove these permissions in your phone's settings, and the rest of the app keeps working.

Backups and exports

If you use "Back up collection", "Export spreadsheet", or "Price list", Grail Case creates a file and opens your phone's share menu. You choose where that file goes, such as your email or cloud drive. Once you send it there, that service's own privacy policy applies to the file. Backup files contain your collection details and values. They do not contain your photos.

Links to other sites

"Check sold prices on eBay" opens eBay in your browser with a search for that piece's name and details. Grail Case does not share anything else with eBay, and we have no connection to eBay. What you do on eBay is covered by eBay's privacy policy.

Purchases

If you buy Grail Case, the payment is handled by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details.

Children

Grail Case is not directed at children under 13, and it does not collect personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date above.
