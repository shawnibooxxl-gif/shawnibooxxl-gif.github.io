HUNT VAULT

Privacy Policy

Effective November 17, 2026

The short version: Hunt Vault does not collect or sell your information. Your hunts, stands, and photos stay on your phone. The only thing the app sends out is your approximate location, to the U.S. National Weather Service, to look up the weather.

Who we are

Hunt Vault is a hunting journal app published by Big Bear LLC, doing business as Big Bear Inc. ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the Android app does with your information.

Information we collect

We do not collect anything. Hunt Vault has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking tools. We do not receive anything you enter into the app.

What is stored on your phone

Your hunts, stands, harvest records, notes, and any photos you attach are saved only in the app's own storage on your device. This data is not uploaded to us or to anyone else.

Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes this information from your phone.

Location and weather

If you allow it, Hunt Vault reads your approximate location while the app is open and sends those coordinates to the U.S. National Weather Service (api.weather.gov) to look up wind, temperature, and pressure. We do not receive, store, or track your location.

The app never uses precise location and never uses location in the background. You can decline the permission and type conditions in yourself. What the Weather Service does with requests is covered by its own policies.

Camera and photos

Hunt Vault asks for camera or photo access only when you choose to add a harvest photo. Photos stay on your phone. You can decline or remove these permissions in your phone's settings, and the rest of the app keeps working.

Exports

If you export your records as a CSV file, Hunt Vault opens your phone's share menu and you choose where the file goes. Once you send it, that service's own privacy policy applies to the file.

Purchases

If you buy Hunt Vault, the payment is handled by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details.

Children

Hunt Vault is not directed at children under 13, and it does not collect personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date above.
