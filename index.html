Privacy

Ammo Vault privacy policy

Last updated October 21, 2026

What we collect

Nothing. Ammo Vault does not collect, transmit, store, sell or share any personal information or usage data. There are no user accounts, analytics, ads or tracking.

Where your data lives

Your inventory, range sessions and notes are stored only on your own device. They are never uploaded to a server, and we cannot see, access or recover them. Deleting the app deletes your data.

Internet and permissions

Ammo Vault works fully offline. It does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files beyond its own local storage.

Children

Ammo Vault is intended for adults. Because it collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.

Changes

If a future version ever changes how data is handled, such as adding an optional backup, we'll update this policy before that version is released.
